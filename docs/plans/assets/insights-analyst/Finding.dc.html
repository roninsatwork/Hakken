Google sends the same number of visitors, but they've stopped enquiring

ronins.co.uk · Found 10 October 2026 · Read from Google Analytics and Search Console

LikelyChecked by the EditorNewSame visitors, fewer results
Add the steps to Hakken tasks

In the last 90 days Google sent 1,371 visitors, against 1,387 in the same weeks last year, and they made 21 enquiries instead of 59. Direct visitors made 23 against 30, so the enquiry form works: what changed is who Google sends. Your guides grew to 42% of all visits and turn 0.7% of them into enquiries, while the pages that brought work faded: chatbots from 22 enquiries a year to 6, blockchain from 13 to 3, the accessibility guide from 15 to 1.
