Measurement check

Before Hakken advises on conversion, it checks the figures can be trusted: every enquiry counted, every visit on a page, and Google and Analytics agreeing.

Four things need a look. The biggest: only form enquiries are counted. Phone, email and live-chat clicks haven't registered once in 12 months, so any enquiry made that way is invisible. Enquiries also have no value, so Hakken counts them rather than pricing them.
