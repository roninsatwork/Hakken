03 · The quarter

Growth came from readers,
not buyers

Our guides now bring 42% of all visits and almost no work. The pages that used to win work lost it as buyers changed the words they search with.

Went well

Our AI agency page is our best: 46 enquiries a year, up from 32. We are first on Google for "ai agency", searched 9,900 times a month.

Didn't

The chatbot page fell from 22 enquiries a year to 6, the blockchain page from 13 to 3, and Clutch from 21 to 6.

Why

Buyers now search for AI agents and automation, not chatbots, and Google sends more readers of our guides and fewer buyers.
