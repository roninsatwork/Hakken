01 · Summary

Winning the market,
losing the enquiries

Marketing brought in 48 enquiries this quarter, down from 110 in the same weeks last year, while our share of the market's Google visitors rose from 3% to 16%. We are third of the five agencies we compete with, up two places. The growth came from readers of our guides, not buyers. Next quarter we refocus on the pages that win work.
