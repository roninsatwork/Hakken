Credit prices

What each kind of work costs a company in credits, and whether each price covers what that work really costs us.

Prices never change by themselves. Each suggestion is what that work really cost over the last 30 days, divided by what a credit covers, rounded up. Use one and save, and it applies from each company’s next run; charges already made keep the price they were made at.

How prices are suggested

Our suppliers charge in dollars. 0.05 means one credit pays for 5 cents of what the work costs us.
